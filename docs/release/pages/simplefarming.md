# Simple Farming

Simple Farming makes common farming chores smoother: Feather Falling protects farmland, Sharpness cuts grass, and a hoe harvests and replants ripe crops.

## Features

- **Feather Falling farmland protection** - Feather Falling boots prevent trampling.
- **Hoe harvest and replant** - Main-hand hoes harvest mature wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart, and cocoa.
- **Cut through grass**
- **Commands** - Permission level 2: /simplefarming farmlandProtect, hoeHarvest and sharpnessCut <true|false>; changes are saved to config/simplefarming.json.
- **Sub-mod of Simple QoL** - Playable alone (config/simplefarming.json).

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, Cloth Config
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Quality of Life, Mod Menu
