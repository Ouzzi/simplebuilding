## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Chess

Simple Chess adds chess pieces (pawn, rook, knight, bishop, queen and king in 13 colors, each as a 3D figure and as a flat token) that stand on a quarter of a block top, so a 4 x 4 area of quartz checkers is a whole chessboard. Twelve quartz checker blocks come with stairs and slabs; with Simple Octets every color also gets an octet.

## Features

- **Chess pieces on checker boards** - 156 chess pieces - pawn, rook, knight, bishop, queen and king in 13 colors, each as a 3D figure and as a flat token.
- **Quartz checkers**
- **Chess octets (with Simple Octets)** - With Simple Octets installed, every color has an octet - a half-block cube; the stonecutter cuts 8 from its checker (quartz: from a quartz block).
- **Moved from SimpleBuilding**

## Vanilla use

Gives builders decoration and real board games on their builds; it only adds its own blocks, items and recipes and changes no Vanilla behavior.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Octets

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Standalone mod.** It works on its own; other Simple mods add optional extras when installed together.
