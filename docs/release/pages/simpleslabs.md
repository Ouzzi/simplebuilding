# Simple Slabs

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Slabs adds a half slab (a quarter of a slab, 2 pixels) for each of the 170 blocks with a slab. Any of the eight layers of a block cell can be filled, so you build 4-pixel ledges, gaps and thin roofs; sneaking puts it upright against a wall as a side half slab or, four layers thick, a side slab. Sand and gravel half slabs fall, grass is tinted by the biome, copper ages. Cut in the Shapecutter (or stonecutter), crafted back into the slab.

## Features

- **Half slabs: 2-pixel layers of every block with a slab** - A half slab is a quarter of a slab: 2 pixels high.
- **Side half slab and side slab** - Sneak + right-click on the side of a block puts the half slab upright against that side: a side half slab of 2 pixels.
- **Slab and half slab in one block** - A half slab used on its own slab (bottom or top, not double) adds a layer: the slab becomes the half slab block with its four layers and the new one, water stays.
- **Breaking and water** - Breaking a half slab block drops one half slab for every filled layer.
- **Sand, gravel and grass**
- **Copper half slabs age** - Unwaxed copper half slabs age like Vanilla copper: exposed, weathered, oxidized.
- **Shapecutter and back** - The Shapecutter of Simple Blocks cuts 4 half slabs from a slab and 8 from its full block; without Simple Blocks the Vanilla stonecutter does it.
- **Half slabs of other mods' blocks**

## Vanilla use

Adds thin building pieces cut from blocks that already have a slab, for finer detail; it only adds its own blocks and recipes and changes no Vanilla block or recipe.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Blocks, Simple End, Simple Ores, Simple Enchanting, Simple Chess, JEI

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
