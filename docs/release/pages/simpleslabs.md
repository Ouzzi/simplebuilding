# Simple Slabs

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Slabs adds a half slab (half a slab, 4 pixels) for every Vanilla block with a slab, plus the slabs of other Simple mods while those are installed. Any of the four layers of a block cell can be filled in any combination, so you build 4-pixel ledges, gaps and thin roofs; the side half slab, an item of its own, stands upright against a wall and grows to a side slab at two layers. Sand and gravel half slabs fall and grass is tinted by the biome (both with Simple Blocks); copper ages. Cut in the Shapecutter (or stonecutter), crafted back into the slab.

## Features

- **Half slabs: 4-pixel layers of every block with a slab** - A half slab is half a slab: 4 pixels high.
- **Side half slab and side slab** - The side half slab is an item of its own for every material: right-click on the side of a block puts it upright against that side, 4 pixels thin.
- **Slab and half slab in one block** - A half slab used on its own slab (bottom or top, not double) adds a layer: the slab becomes the half slab block with its two layers and the new one, water stays.
- **Breaking and water** - Breaking a half slab block drops one half slab for every filled layer, a side form one side half slab per layer.
- **Sand, gravel and grass** - Sand, red sand and gravel half slabs fall like sand.
- **Copper half slabs age** - Unwaxed copper half slabs age like Vanilla copper: exposed, weathered, oxidized.
- **Shapecutter and back** - The Shapecutter of Simple Blocks cuts 2 half slabs or 2 side half slabs from a slab and 4 from its full block; without Simple Blocks the Vanilla stonecutter does it.
- **Half slabs of other mods' blocks** - Half slabs of another mod's blocks (SimpleBuilding, Simple Blocks, Simple End, Simple Ores, Simple Enchanting) exist only while that mod is loaded, with their recipes.

## Vanilla use

Adds thin building pieces cut from blocks that already have a slab, for finer detail; it only adds its own blocks and recipes and changes no Vanilla block or recipe.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API (no other mods)
- **Included in this download:** SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Blocks, Simple End, Simple Ores, Simple Enchanting, JEI

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding: included in that download, and it also runs on its own with the required mods above.
