# Simple Ores

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Ores adds silver ore in the Overworld and the End, two gold-copper-silver alloys (hard Rose Copper, soft Rose Gold), pressings, tools and armor of their own, Enderite (the tier above Netherite), Dimensional Scrap and Sage Ore, building blocks, doors, lamps and plates of eight building metals, and in the End the Astralit and Nihilit ores with two redstone lines of their own (Astral and Nihil Redstone, rails, pistons and lamps).

## Features

- **Silver** - A pale metal found in the Overworld and the End.
- **Rose gold** - Two alloys of gold, copper and silver: Rose Gold and Rose Copper, which is made from Rose Gold.
- **Rose gold tools and armor** - Sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and a full armor set of each alloy.
- **Rose gold apples** - A plain and an enchanted apple of each alloy, between the golden apple and the Netherite apple.
- **Lead** - A dull, heavy metal of the stone layer and the End.
- **Zinc, nickel, tin and their alloys** - Three more metals of the Overworld and their plain alloys brass, stainless steel and bronze.
- **Metal building blocks** - Silver, lead, zinc, nickel, tin and brass are building metals only - no tools, no armor.
- **Soundproofing** - Lead walls make the world quieter on the other side: a TNT blast behind a few lead blocks is only a dull thud, or not heard at all.
- **Silver mirror** - A decoration of silver and glass for walls, floors and ceilings.
- **Stacking plates** - Lead plates and silver sheets stack like snow layers: up to 8 layers in one cell.
- **Metal bars and grates**
- **Metal doors and trapdoors** - Every building metal - silver, lead, zinc, nickel, tin, brass, bronze and stainless steel - has a door and a trapdoor like the copper ones.
