## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Ores

Simple Ores adds silver ore in the Overworld and the End, two gold-copper-silver alloys (hard Rose Copper, soft Rose Gold), pressings, tools and armor of their own, Enderite (the tier above Netherite), Dimensional Scrap and Sage Ore.

## Features

- **Silver** - A pale metal found in the Overworld and the End.
- **Rose gold** - Two alloys of gold, copper and silver: Rose Gold and the stronger-gold Rose Copper, which is made from Rose Gold.
- **Rose gold tools and armor** - Sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and a full armor set of each alloy.
- **Rose gold apples** - A plain and an enchanted apple of each alloy, between the golden apple and the Netherite apple.
- **Lead** - A dull, heavy metal of the stone layer and the End.
- **Zinc, nickel, tin and their alloys** - Three more metals of the Overworld and their plain alloys brass, stainless steel and bronze.
- **Metal building blocks**
- **Soundproofing** - Lead walls make the world quieter on the other side: a TNT blast behind a few lead blocks is only a dull thud, or not heard at all.
- **Silver mirror** - A decoration of silver and glass for walls, floors and ceilings.
- **Stacking plates** - Lead plates and silver sheets stack like snow layers: up to 8 layers in one cell.
- **Metal bars and grates**
- **Metal sounds** - Every building metal sounds like itself when placed, mined, hit or walked on - quietly.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Hammers

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding, Simple Pads. It is a required part of the super mod, and it loads without it as long as its own required mods above are installed.
