## Description (Markdown)

# Simple End

Simple End adds Ashwood and Duskwood with buds, trees and stick scrub, End grass, and the Ash Valley and Dawnlands biomes on the outer End islands. It bundles Simple End Dragon Fight.

## Features

- **Ashwood and Duskwood**
- **Buds, gigantic End trees and stick trees** - Ash Bud and Dusk Bud grow on End stone or dirt.
- **Leaves, Drift Fruit and Spoiled Drift Fruit** - End leaves are no full block: see-through tendril fronds around a small crystal, tinted by the biome.
- **Void-safe wood**
- **End grass and End bushes**
- **Ash Valley and Dawnlands**
- **Nibblemite** - A small, passive Endermite kin that lives in Ash Valley and Dawnlands in groups of 3-5, colored after its biome (pale gray with lavender, taupe with rose).
- **Simple End and its sub-mod** - SimpleBuilding bundles Simple End ("Enable Simple End"); Simple End bundles Simple End Dragon Fight ("Enable Simple End Dragon Fight" in config/simpleend.json).
- **Commands** - /simpleend config shows and changes every option of config/simpleend.json (list [group], get, set, reset, help; Tab completes options and values).

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API (no other mods)
- **Optional:** Simple End Dragon Fight

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Standalone mod.** It works on its own; other Simple mods add optional extras when installed together.
