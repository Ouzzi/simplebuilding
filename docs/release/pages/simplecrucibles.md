# Simple Crucibles

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Crucibles adds Iron, Reinforced, Netherite and Enderite crucibles (a cooking station without fuel, heated by the block below; the Enderite tier needs Simple Ores), the reinforced cauldron, copper and soul lava buckets, soul lava in the Nether and village field kitchens. Barrels of Simple Storage dock onto a crucible.

## Features

- **Crucibles** - A cooking station without fuel that cooks every slot at once, heated by the block below.
- **Village field kitchens** - Villages of all five types can have a small field kitchen: an iron crucible on an unlit campfire with cooked food, a barrel of raw meat and a seat.
- **Reinforced cauldron** - A cauldron for water, lava, powder snow and soul lava; lava in it heats a crucible like a lava source.
- **Netherite and Enderite cauldron** - Reinforced cauldrons with room for 2 (Netherite) or 4 (Enderite) buckets of one content.
- **Soul lava** - A rare Nether fluid and the only extreme heat source: it flows short and slow, is never replaced, and gives Soul Burn.
- **Copper and soul lava buckets** - Buckets for the crucible's fluids: the iron bucket breaks on soul lava, copper oxidizes.
- **Recipe book** - Every craftable item of the mod is in the recipe book: picking up one of its ingredients unlocks the recipe.
- **Creative tabs** - The module's items sit in its own creative tab only (no Vanilla tabs).
- **Commands** - /simplecrucibles config shows and changes every option of config/simplecrucibles-server.json (list [group], get, set, reset, help; Tab completes options and values).

## Vanilla use

Adds a fuel-free cooking station for the mid game, a sturdier cauldron and soul lava, a rare Nether fluid in the world generation (can be switched off); the iron bucket breaks when it pours soul lava, and copper buckets carry water and lava (never soul lava) and also work on the Vanilla cauldron.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API (no other mods)
- **Included in this download:** SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, JEI, Jade, Simple Storage, Simple Ores, Simple Tools

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding: included in that download, and it also runs on its own with the required mods above.
