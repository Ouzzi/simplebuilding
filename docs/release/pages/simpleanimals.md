## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Animals

Simple Animals adds deer, wild boar, hedgehog, crab and zebra (with the infertile zorse). They use Vanilla goals and spawn rarely.

## Features

- **Deer** - A shy herd animal of forests and taiga with red and fallow coats; bucks carry antlers.
- **Wild boar** - Neutral, like the polar bear: an adult attacks a player who comes too close to one of its piglets.
- **Hedgehog**
- **Crab** - A harmless beach animal with a red or sand shell and two big claws; it walks sideways.
- **Zebra** - A horse of the savanna with its own looks: classic black-and-white striped coat and a stiff mane.
- **Zorse** - Zebra x horse: a brown rideable horse with zebra stripes on legs and rump.
- **Spawning**
- **Commands** - /simpleanimals config shows and changes every option of config/simpleanimals-server.json (list [group], get, set, reset, help; Tab completes options and values).

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API (no other mods)
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Money, Simple Mobheads

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Standalone mod.** It works on its own; other Simple mods add optional extras when installed together.
