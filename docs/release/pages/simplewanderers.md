## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Wanderers

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Wanderers adds one Wandering Trader per village with a leashed horse. Goods are split from the traveling trader, with a new stock every 3 days and a restock every 1.5 days. Besides Fabric API it only needs SimpleLib; no other Simple mod is required.

## Features

- **Village Wanderer** - One travel-cloaked trader per village: a Wandering Trader in an ochre cloak and a hat with a wide brim.
- **Wanderer's horse** - A normal horse in an ochre leather armor hangs on the wanderer's leash.
- **New stock and restock** - A fresh set of offers every 3 days and a restock every 1.5 days.
- **Goods: wanderer and traveling trader** - The goods of the Wandering Trader are split.
- **Biome assortment**
- **Commands** - /simplewanderers config shows and changes every option of config/simplewanderers-server.json (list [group], get, set, reset, help; Tab completes options and values).

## Vanilla use

Gives every village a wandering trader with a horse and, in return, moves the everyday half (47 of 78) of the Vanilla Wandering Trader's goods to him; the traveling trader keeps the rest, plus anything other mods add to the Vanilla trade lists. Off in the config, the Vanilla trader is untouched.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Money, Simple Mobheads

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Standalone mod.** It works on its own; other Simple mods add optional extras when installed together.
