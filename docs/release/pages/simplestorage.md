## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Storage

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Storage adds crates, the Storage Crafting Table, tiered and trapped chests, tier shulker boxes and barrels with more slots and bigger stacks, chest carts and chest boats, bundles, quivers, backpacks, item pipes, a chest network with a controller chest, and the Drawer, Deep Pockets and Funnel enchantments. New structures sometimes hold a better chest or a rare tiered shulker. Worlds from earlier SimpleBuilding versions keep their crates and Storage Crafting Tables.

## Features

- **Crates** - Crates are open wooden boxes shaped like the composter that store up to 9 stacks of food.
- **Storage Crafting Table** - The Storage Crafting Table is a crafting table that keeps its 3x3 grid: closing the screen leaves the items where they are, and they lie flat on the table's top.
- **Chest tiers** - Copper chest -> Reinforced Chest -> Netherite Chest -> Enderite Chest: more slots per tier and bigger stacks from netherite on.
- **Shulker box tiers** - Shulker box -> Reinforced -> Netherite -> Enderite Shulker Box: the slots and stack sizes of the chest tiers in a box that keeps everything when broken.
- **Barrels** - Copper, Reinforced, Netherite and Enderite barrels with the slots and stacks of the chest tiers, upgraded in place.
- **Bundles: Reinforced, Netherite, Enderite** - Three bundle tiers above the vanilla bundle: Reinforced 96 items, Netherite 192 and Enderite 288.
- **Quivers** - Quivers are arrow bundles that take only arrows and supply a bow automatically.
- **Placed bundles** - Sneak + right-click on a block with a bundle or quiver to set it down as a 3D bundle - on the floor (falls like sand) or hung on a wall.
- **Backpacks** - Backpacks are storage you wear in the chest slot: four tiers from 9 to 50 slots, opened with their own key (default B) as your normal inventory plus the backpack's slots.
- **Storage enchantments: Deep Pockets, Drawer, Funnel** - Three enchantments for bundles, quivers and backpacks: Deep Pockets, Drawer and Funnel.
- **Pipes** - A pipe is a hopper with one slot that looks like a cable.
- **Chest network** - Copper cables connect chests, each with a chest interface on its side, to one controller chest.

## Vanilla use

Gives players more storage (crates, tiered chests and shulker boxes, bundles, quivers, backpacks); the Vanilla containers stay, and some mixins touch chest, shulker, bundle and arrow pickup behavior.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API (no other mods)
- **Included in this download:** SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple End, Trinkets Updated, Curios, Simple Ores, Simple Crucibles

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding: included in that download, and it also runs on its own with the required mods above.
