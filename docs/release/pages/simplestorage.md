## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Storage

Simple Storage adds crates, the Storage Crafting Table, tiered and trapped chests, tier shulker boxes with more slots and bigger stacks, barrels, chest carts and chest boats, bundles, quivers, backpacks, and the Drawer, Deep Pockets and Funnel enchantments. Ids from older versions migrate.

## Features

- **Crates**
- **Storage Crafting Table** - The Storage Crafting Table is a crafting table that keeps its 3x3 grid: closing the screen leaves the items where they are, and they lie flat on the table's top.
- **Moved from SimpleBuilding** - The crates of every wood and the Storage Crafting Table, their block entities, recipes and recipe unlocks were SimpleBuilding's (simplebuilding:*).
- **Creative tabs** - Own tab with the Storage Crafting Table, the crates and the guide.
- **Chest tiers**
- **Shulker box tiers**
- **Rare structure finds** - In newly generated structures a loot chest is now and then a better chest with double loot, and end city shulkers are sometimes reinforced, netherite or enderite.
- **Bundles: Reinforced, Netherite, Enderite**
- **Quivers**
- **Placed bundles** - Sneak + right-click on a block with a bundle or quiver to set it down as a 3D bundle - on the floor (falls like sand) or hung on a wall.
- **Backpacks** - Backpacks are storage you wear in the chest slot: four tiers from 9 to 50 slots, opened with their own key (default B) as your normal inventory plus the backpack's slots.
- **Storage enchantments: Deep Pockets, Drawer, Funnel**

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple End, Trinkets Updated, Curios, Simple Ores, Simple Crucibles

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding. It is a required part of the super mod, and it loads without it as long as its own required mods above are installed.
