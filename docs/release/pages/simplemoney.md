# Simple Money

Simple Money adds a currency you can produce, weighted villager trades and money in treasure loot.

## Features

- **Special Paper** - Crafted from two paper and one honeycomb; used with an iron ingot and resin fiber to smith a banknote blank.
- **Special Fiber** - Crafted from copper nuggets, gold nuggets, amethyst shards, and diamonds; used with a gold ingot to refine a banknote blank.
- **Resin Fiber** - Crafted from iron nuggets, resin clumps, honeycomb, and bone meal; used to smith banknote blanks.
- **Banknote Blank** - An uncommon smithing ingredient made from special paper, resin fiber, and an iron ingot.
- **Refined Banknote Blank** - An uncommon ingredient made by refining a banknote blank.
- **Raw Bill** - A rare crafting result.
- **Money Bill** - Currency for the added villager and wandering trader offers.
- **Trade: armorer/25** - Additional offer; existing Vanilla and SimpleBuilding offers remain in the pool.
- **Trade: armorer/26** - Additional offer; existing Vanilla and SimpleBuilding offers remain in the pool.
- **Trade: armorer/27** - Additional offer; existing Vanilla and SimpleBuilding offers remain in the pool.
- **Trade: armorer/28** - Additional offer; existing Vanilla and SimpleBuilding offers remain in the pool.
- **Trade: armorer/29** - Additional offer; existing Vanilla and SimpleBuilding offers remain in the pool.

## Vanilla use

Adds a currency, weighted villager trades and treasure loot so survival worlds get an economy; Vanilla trades are not removed.
