## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Money

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Money adds a currency you can produce, weighted villager trades and money in treasure loot.

## Features

- **Special Paper** - Crafted from two paper and one honeycomb; used with an iron ingot and resin fiber to smith a banknote blank.
- **Special Fiber** - Crafted from copper nuggets, gold nuggets, amethyst shards, and diamonds; used with a gold ingot to refine a banknote blank.
- **Resin Fiber** - Crafted from iron nuggets, resin clumps, honeycomb, and bone meal; used to smith banknote blanks.
- **Banknote Blank** - An uncommon smithing ingredient made from special paper, resin fiber, and an iron ingot.
- **Refined Banknote Blank** - An uncommon ingredient made by refining a banknote blank.
- **Raw Bill** - A rare crafting result.
- **Money Bill** - Currency for the added villager and wandering trader offers.
- **One price logic** - All 47 own offers follow one rule: one money bill is worth 3 diamonds or 16 emeralds (making a bill costs two diamonds plus gold, iron, resin and blast furnace time).
- **Treasure currency** - One independent roll per chest.
- **Server configuration**
- **Experimental Forge 26.3** - Opt-in Forge adapter with shared items, recipes, trades, loot, JSON configuration, and the same server test catalog.
- **Money Links: Add-on Trades** - Adds buy-only money offers for loaded mods without removing their original trades.

## Vanilla use

Adds a currency, weighted villager trades and treasure loot so survival worlds get an economy. Making a bill is deliberately expensive (two diamonds plus gold, iron and resin each), so money is a sink for diamonds and gold, not a loophole. Villagers and the wandering trader get extra offers (a few Vanilla goods for bills, goods of other Simple mods for bills, bills for diamonds); Vanilla offers stay in the pool and no Vanilla loot table is replaced. Offers that remove Vanilla scarcity (budding amethyst, diamond ore) are off by default.
