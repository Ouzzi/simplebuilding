## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Layers

Simple Layers adds the Cloud Sea above y 700 and the Backrooms below the bedrock, with cloud blocks, wallpaper, carpet and neon lights.

## Features

- **Cloud blocks** - Cloud Block, Cloud Stone and Mist Block break instantly, by hand too.
- **The Cloud Sea** - Rise above y 700 in the Overworld (config clouds.entryY, 400-1000) and you enter the Cloud Sea, a sky dimension of floating islands over the same x/z.
- **The Backrooms**
- **The quick way out**
- **Ten levels, one below the other**
- **Heights and rooms** - The rooms are not all alike.
- **Chalk** - Chalk marks your way - in the Backrooms, in caves, in any mine.
- **Harder ways out** - In the Pipes every region has a sealed hatch with three valve levers beside it.
- **Abandoned house**
- **Sounds** - The Backrooms have no constant hum.
- **Walls, hull and supplies**
- **The Listener** - Option backrooms.listener (off by default): now and then footsteps sound behind you in the Backrooms, come closer and stop.

## Dependencies

- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding
