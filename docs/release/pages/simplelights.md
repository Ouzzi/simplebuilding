## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Lights

Simple Lights adds 1 px lamps for any block side: plain, transparent, sixteen colors, a redstone pane whose color follows the signal strength, and copper panes that toggle like the Copper Bulb.

## Features

- **Light Panes**
- **Moved from SimpleBuilding** - The 37 Light Panes, their recipes and recipe unlocks were SimpleBuilding's (simplebuilding:*).
- **Creative tabs** - The module's items sit in its own creative tab only (no Vanilla tabs).

## Vanilla use

Adds thin lamps that can sit on any block side so builders can light rooms neatly; Vanilla lamps are unchanged.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding. It is a required part of the super mod, and it loads without it as long as its own required mods above are installed.
