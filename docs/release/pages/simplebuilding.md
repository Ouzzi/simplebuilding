# SimpleBuilding

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

SimpleBuilding is the core of the Simple family. It bundles the required sub-mods (building wands, chisels, sledgehammers, storage, ovens, crucibles, building blocks, gadgets, pads and more) and adds its own content on top: End building palettes, Astral and Nihil redstone lines, Enderite tools and armor, enchantments for mining and building, music discs and hammocks. Optional Simple mods (for example Simple Maps or Simple Money) are separate downloads and are not part of this file; each bundled sub-mod can be switched off in the config.

## Vanilla use

Adds tools, blocks and gear on top of a normal survival world, but it also reaches into Vanilla: its pools join the loot of 14 kinds of structure chests and of fishing treasure (switchable per structure, scalable with one factor); its offers join the villagers and the wandering trader (switchable); world generation adds two End ores, four small End structures, cracked-ice fields and sculk jaws (ores and ice fields switchable); new armor stands get arms and swap armor with you, sneaking with small items such as sticks or ingots places them on blocks, XP orbs merge, and repairs without a new enchantment keep the anvil cost (each switchable); hammocks speed up the time of day while enough players rest in them (factor 1 turns it off).
