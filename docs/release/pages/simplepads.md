## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Pads

Simple Pads adds the Launchpad, Elytra Pad, Flypad, Potion Pad, Spawn Teleporter and Chunk Loader in three tiers each, plus Diamond, Netherite, Enderite and copper pressure plates and the Spawn Elytra.

## Features

- **Simple Pads: pads, pressure plates and the Spawn Elytra**

## Vanilla use

Adds launch, flight, potion and teleport pads plus stronger pressure plates for travel and automation; Vanilla elytra and wind charges work as before, with small hooks for the spawn elytra.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib, Simple Ores, Simple Mobheads
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Jade

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding. It is a required part of the super mod, and it loads without it as long as its own required mods above are installed.
