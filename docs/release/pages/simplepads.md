## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Pads

> **Beta.** This mod is released as a beta: features, balance and config options can still change between versions, and bugs are possible. Back up your world before you install or update it, and report problems on the issue tracker.

Simple Pads adds the Launchpad, Elytra Pad, Flypad, Potion Pad, Spawn Teleporter and Chunk Loader in three tiers each, plus Diamond, Netherite, Enderite and copper pressure plates and the Spawn Elytra.

## Features

- **Simple Pads: tiers, upgrades and settings**
- **Elytra Pads**
- **Flypads**
- **Spawn Teleporters** - Spawn Teleporters take you to your own respawn point, or to the shared spawn target when powered by redstone.
- **Launchpads** - Launchpads (from Simple Tweaks) are charged with wind charges and launch you after 3 seconds of standing on them; the more charges, the higher.
- **Chunk Loaders**
- **Potion Pads** - Potion Pads store the effects of a thrown potion and give them to every player who steps onto them.
- **Pressure plates: Diamond, Netherite, Enderite Owner Plate, Copper**
- **Spawn Elytra**

## Vanilla use

Adds launch, flight, potion and teleport pads plus stronger pressure plates for travel and automation; Vanilla elytra and wind charges work as before, with small hooks for the spawn elytra.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, Simple Mobheads, Simple Ores
- **Included in this download:** SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Jade

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding: included in that download, and it also runs on its own with the required mods above.
