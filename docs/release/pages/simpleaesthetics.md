# Simple Aesthetics

Simple Aesthetics has no content of its own. It bundles Simple Visuals, Simple Sounds and Simple Models with one on/off switch each.

## Features

- **Super-mod for looks and sounds** - Simple Aesthetics has no content of its own: it bundles Simple Visuals, Simple Sounds and Simple Models.
- **Commands** - /simpleaesthetics config shows and changes every option of config/simpleaesthetics.json (list [group], get, set, reset, help; Tab completes options and values).

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, Simple Visuals, Simple Sounds, Simple Models
- **Optional:** none

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Super mod.** It requires its sub-mods (Simple Visuals, Simple Sounds, Simple Models); each sub-mod can be switched off in the config ("Enable Simple XY") and each can also be installed without the super mod.
