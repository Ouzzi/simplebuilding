## Description (Markdown)

# Simple Enchanting

Simple Enchanting adds the Dark Enchanter (three random enchantments with sliders up to Vanilla's maximum) and the Astral Enchanter (higher tier, Protection V and Feather Falling V). Both are made in the world with a hammer and nuggets.

## Features

- **Dark and Astral Enchanter** - Pick your enchantments yourself: three random ones that fit the item, each with a slider up to its maximum, paid from a level budget the shelves and the floor set.
- **Floors and shelves** - SimpleBuilding owns the building blocks the tables need: Blazing and Nihil Obsidian, blazewood planks and bookshelves.
- **Creative tabs** - The module's items sit in its own creative tab only (no Vanilla tabs).
- **Commands** - /simpleenchanting config [list | get | set | reset | help] (operators).

## Vanilla use

Gives players two upgraded enchanting tables for higher tiers; the Vanilla enchanting table works as before.

## Dependencies

- **Required:** Fabric API, SimpleLib
- **Optional:** SimpleBuilding, Simple Ores, Simple Hammers

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Java 25
- Fabric only for now.

## Part of the Simple family

**Sub-mod** of SimpleBuilding. It is a required part of the super mod, and it loads without it as long as its own required mods above are installed.
