## Release notes

First release of **Simple Enchanting** for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric.

Two enchanters above Vanilla's table: the Dark Enchanter (three random enchantments with sliders up to Vanilla's maximum, tier 50 with blazewood bookshelves and an obsidian floor) and the Astral Enchanter (tier 60 on Nihil Obsidian, Protection V and Feather Falling V). Made in the world with a hammer and eight nuggets. Works on its own; the Astral Enchanter needs Simple Ores.

## Highlights (from the commit history)

- End bookshelves, chiseled bookshelves with Vanilla book variation, Nihilit Book, mod books in chiseled shelves
- Dark Enchanter subtle glow animation (b-ench03, )
- Simple Enchanting sub-mod - Dark Enchanter (old Astral table, id moved) and new Astral Enchanter, Nihil Obsidian

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API
- Required mods: SimpleLib
- Optional integrations: simplebuilding, simpleores, simplehammers

## Notes

- All options are in the config screen (Mod Menu / Cloth Config) and in `/simpleenchanting config` where available.
- Back up your world before updating from an older SimpleBuilding build; old ids are migrated automatically.
