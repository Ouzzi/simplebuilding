# Simple Animals 0.1.0 - changelog (Minecraft 26.3, Fabric)

## Release notes

First release of **Simple Animals** for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric.

Five animals Vanilla does not have: the shy deer, the wild boar that defends its piglets, the hedgehog that hunts silverfish and rolls up in a flash, the rare sideways-walking crab and the zebra, a striped horse whose cross with a horse is the infertile zorse. Works on its own with Vanilla.

## Added

- **Deer** - A shy herd animal of forests and taiga with red and fallow coats; bucks carry antlers.
- **Wild boar** - Neutral, like the polar bear: an adult attacks a player who comes too close to one of its piglets.
- **Hedgehog** - A small animal of forests and meadows that hunts silverfish within 8 blocks and rolls up when threatened, as the armadillo does, but much faster: 0.3 seconds instead of about 1 second.
- **Crab** - A harmless beach animal with a red or sand shell and two big claws; it walks sideways.
- **Zebra** - A horse of the savanna with its own looks: classic black-and-white striped coat and a stiff mane.
- **Zorse** - Zebra x horse: a brown rideable horse with zebra stripes on legs and rump.
- **Spawning** - All natural spawns are low next to Vanilla (sheep 12, pig 10, cow 8), only in daylight on grass (the crab on sand or mud) and never in caves; spawn eggs, spawners and breeding are not limited.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API
- Optional integrations: simplebuilding, simplemoney, simplemobheads

## Notes

- All options are in the config screen (Mod Menu / Cloth Config) and in `/simpleanimals config` where available.
- Back up your world before updating from an older SimpleBuilding build; old ids are migrated automatically.
