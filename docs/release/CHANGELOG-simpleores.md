# Simple Ores 0.1.0 - changelog (Minecraft 26.3, Fabric)

## Release notes

First release of **Simple Ores** for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric.

Silver ore in the Overworld and the End, lead (blocks that muffle sound and stop the Sculk), the silver mirror, and rose gold: two alloys of gold, copper and silver (hard Rose Copper, soft Rose Gold), pressed into pressings, melted in the blast furnace only, with tools and armor of their own. Works on its own with Vanilla.

## Added

- **Silver** - A pale metal found in the Overworld and the End.
- **Rose gold** - Two alloys of gold, copper and silver: Rose Gold and the stronger-gold Rose Copper, which is made from Rose Gold.
- **Rose gold tools and armor** - Sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and a full armor set of each alloy.
- **Rose gold apples** - A plain and an enchanted apple of each alloy, between the golden apple and the Netherite apple.
- **Lead** - A dull, heavy metal of the stone layer and the End.
- **Zinc, nickel, tin and their alloys** - Three more metals of the Overworld and their plain alloys brass, stainless steel and bronze.
- **Metal building blocks** - Silver, lead, zinc, nickel, tin and brass are building metals only - no tools, no armor (owner 09.11.).
- **Soundproofing** - Lead walls make the world quieter on the other side: a TNT blast behind a few lead blocks is only a dull thud, or not heard at all.
- **Silver mirror** - A wall decoration of silver and glass.
- **Enderite** - The tier above netherite (moved here from SimpleLib.
- **Nether Rose Gold Ore** - A rare rose gold find hidden in the Nether gold veins, deep down in the heat.
- **Sage Crystal** - The ingot-sized material of the sage family, found as shards in the Nether and the End.
- **Sage Ore and Dimensional Scrap** - Two rare finds that moved here from SimpleBuilding: the sage ore with its experience orb, and the scrap of the three dimensions.
- **Rift alloys** - The Dimensional Scrap of each dimension melts into rift shards; with a partner ingot they make Terranite, Infernite and Zenithite, a chain through all three dimensions that starts at Enderite.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API
- Required mods: SimpleLib
- Optional integrations: simplebuilding, simplehammers

## Notes

- All options are in the config screen (Mod Menu / Cloth Config) and in `/simpleores config` where available.
- Back up your world before updating from an older SimpleBuilding build; old ids are migrated automatically.
