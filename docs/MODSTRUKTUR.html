Simple Arrowsneu
simplearrows

Arrows: the Fletching Table (tip, shaft and fletching, an optional potion: 4 arrows with their own damage and flight), the Light Arrow, which lights the spot where it sticks until it despawns like a normal arrow, and the Silver Arrow, which hits undead harder (crafted with Simple Ores' silver). Works on its own.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Ores
Simple Blocksfertig
simpleblocks

Missing building blocks: Hushed Deepslate Tiles that dampen vibrations like wool, Sculk Deepslate, the missing Vanilla stairs, slabs and walls, cracked/mossy brick variants, suspended and levitating sand and gravel, dirt, grass, sand and gravel slabs, cracked and chiseled ice, the nautilus shell block and three more froglight colors. The Shapecutter cuts stone and wood into every stair, slab and shape. Works on its own with Vanilla.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, jei
Simple Building Toolsneu
simplebuildingtools

Building tools of the SimpleBuilding family: Building Wands (copper to Enderite and creative), the Octant in 17 colors and the Blueprint with editor, picture import and cartography table copies. Simple Ores adds the Enderite wand, SimpleBuilding the backpack supply and the placed octants.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Ores, Simple Storage, Simple Hammers, Simple Octets
Simple Chiselsneu
simplechisels

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Ores, jei
Simple Enchantingneu
simpleenchanting

Two enchanters above Vanilla's table: the Dark Enchanter (three random enchantments with sliders up to Vanilla's maximum, tier 50 with blazewood bookshelves and an obsidian floor) and the Astral Enchanter (tier 60 on Nihil Obsidian, Protection V and Feather Falling V). Made in the world with a hammer and eight nuggets. Works on its own; the Astral Enchanter needs Simple Ores.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Ores, Simple Hammers
Simple Gadgetsfertig
simplegadgets

Gadgets you hold and use: the Gauge (speed dial, altimeter with fall-damage warning, auto-walk), the Transposer (turns and swaps blocks in place), the Resonance Rod, the Detector, the Attractor and the Echo Sounder; the Octant lives in Simple Building Tools.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding
Simple Hammersfertig
simplehammers

Seven sledgehammers (Stone to Enderite) that mine a 3x3 area, reshape blocks into stairs and slabs, carve octets, crush diamond and quartz blocks and upgrade blocks in the world strike by strike, with the enchantments Break Through, Override and Radius. While it is loaded the in-world ways of the other Simple mods take the hammer instead of the axe.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Octets, Simple Crucibles, Simple Ores, jei

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding
Simple Machinesneu
simplemachines

The machine parts of the Simple Machines tab: Double Piston (one block, two stages), Signal Dampener (takes 0-3 off the signal), and the piston upgrades - Reinforced (Sticky) Piston (pushes 18, one paid unbreakable), Netherite and Enderite Piston (break the block in front, with durability, repaired with their nugget), Reinforced, Netherite and Enderite Hopper (2/4/8 times as fast, item filter) and their hopper minecarts, the Auto Smither (runs the smithing table on a redstone pulse) and the Auto Crafter (crafts by itself into a hopper below). Works on its own; the Enderite parts need Simple Ores.

required SimpleLib
optional jade, SimpleBuilding, Simple Ores, jei

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Mobs, Simple End, Simple End Dragon Fight, jei, modmenu, cloth_config
Simple Mobsneu
simplemobs

New mobs (Hollow Howler, Hollow Watcher, Shellker, Soulfire Blaze, Dread, Deceiver) and summoned bosses (Void Runner, Brimguard, the armored Blighted and Abyssal Wither). Weathered Trials with the Evil and Void Omen chain (trials, raids, vaults, keys), Wings tiers 1 to 4 with Nether Heart, Red Nether Gem, Ender Heart and Ender Gem, Spawner Fragments and the Makeshift Spawner, Strong Gunpowder, TNT and Rocket, the Trumpet, the Cloak of Deception and Trick Mirror, combat effects, armor trim abilities, the brood hollow with Mob Essence and the Potion of Silence. Requires Simple Resonance.

required SimpleLib, Simple Resonance
Simple Octetsfertig
simpleoctets

Octets: half-block cubes (0.5 x 0.5 x 0.5) of every wood, every block with stairs and slabs and the melon, which mix in one cell. Cut in the Shapecutter of Simple Blocks (in the stonecutter without it), crafted back into the block; the sledgehammer of Simple Hammers carves them and breaks one corner off a full block. Unwaxed copper octets age like Vanilla copper. Melon placing and corner breaking can be switched off (simpleoctets-server.json). Octets of SimpleBuilding's and Simple End's blocks only with that mod.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple End, Simple Ores, Simple Enchanting, jei
Simple Oresneu
simpleores

Silver ore in the Overworld and the End, lead (blocks that muffle sound and stop the Sculk), the silver mirror, and rose gold: two alloys of gold, copper and silver (hard Rose Copper, soft Rose Gold), pressed into pressings, melted in the blast furnace only, with tools and armor of their own. Also Enderite, the tier above netherite (other Simple mods show their Enderite variants only with it), Dimensional Scrap and Sage Ore. Works on its own with Vanilla.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Hammers
Simple Ovensfertig
simpleovens

Reinforced, Netherite and Enderite furnaces, smokers and blast furnaces (2x/4x/8x speed, double experience from Netherite) and furnace minecarts. Upgrades in the world with the axe on its own, with the sledgehammer of Simple Hammers.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Foundry, Simple Crucibles, Simple Ores
Simple Cruciblesfertig
simplecrucibles

Iron, Reinforced, Netherite and Enderite crucibles (a cooking station without fuel; the barrels that dock onto them are Simple Storage's), the reinforced cauldron and village field kitchens. Upgrades in the world with the axe on its own, with the sledgehammer of Simple Hammers.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, jei, jade, Simple Storage, Simple Ores, Simple Tools
Simple Foundryneu
simplefoundry

A multiblock foundry: build a tank of melting chambers in a brick hull, throw in ore and fuel, and pour the melt by layer through spouts into casting trays. Gold, copper and iron, one ingot per ingot. Works on its own with Vanilla; a sub-mod of Simple Ovens.

required SimpleLib
optional Simple Ovens

Simple Resonanceneu
simpleresonance

How experienced a player is and how dangerous the land is: a player value from playing time and survival, danger zones that grow softly with the distance from the spawn, the owner of a monster spawn and a newcomer protection. An API for other mods; allows Simple Mobs to give new monsters status effects instead of more health.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Mobs
Simple Slabsneu
simpleslabs

Half slabs (4 pixels) of every block with a slab: stack them freely in the four 4-pixel layers of a cell, lean them against a wall as side half slab or side slab. Sand, red sand and gravel half slabs fall, grass takes the biome color, copper ages like Vanilla copper. Cut in the Shapecutter of Simple Blocks (in the stonecutter without it), crafted back into the slab. Half slabs of another mod's block only with that mod; shares SimpleLib's shape core with Simple Octets.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Blocks, Simple End, Simple Ores, Simple Enchanting, jei

Simple Optimization: 3 Sub-Mods

Simple Optimizationfertig
simpleoptimization

Super-mod: bundles Simple Opt Render, Simple Opt Memory and Simple Opt Server, with one on/off switch each.

required SimpleLib, Simple Opt Render, Simple Opt Memory, Simple Opt Server
Simple Opt Memoryfertig
simpleoptmemory

Memory optimizations of Simple Optimization: block states share their read-only property and face tables (steps back for FerriteCore); model baking later.

required SimpleLib
Simple Opt Renderfertig
simpleoptrender

Client optimizations of Simple Optimization: leaf culling, light-map cache, more culling (sign text, item frames, beacon beams), fast IP ping, telemetry off, optional detail switches (particles, texture animations, air fog). Each area steps back when a known optimization mod covers it.

required SimpleLib
Simple Opt Serverfertig
simpleoptserver

Server optimizations of Simple Optimization: view distance follows server load (MSPT) within the server.properties bounds; optional activation range for far mobs (off by default); steps back when ServerCore is installed.

required SimpleLib
