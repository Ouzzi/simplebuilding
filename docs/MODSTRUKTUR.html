Simple Blocksfertig
simpleblocks

Missing building blocks: Hushed Deepslate Tiles (dampen vibrations like wool), Sculk Deepslate, the missing Vanilla stairs, slabs and walls and cracked/mossy brick variants, and SimpleBuilding's building blocks without a home of their own (suspended and levitating sand/gravel, dirt/grass/sand/gravel slabs, cracked and chiseled ice, the nautilus shell block, three froglight colors; ids migrate). The Shapecutter cuts stone and wood into every stair, slab and shape. Works on its own with Vanilla; required sub-mod of SimpleBuilding.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, jei

simplecrucibles

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, jei, jade
Simple Gadgetsfertig
simplegadgets

Gadgets you hold and use - for now the Gauge (speed dial, altimeter with fall-damage warning, auto-walk), moved from SimpleBuilding (ids migrate); the Transposer, Resonance Rod, Detector, Attractor, Echo Sounder, Color Brush and Octant follow stage by stage. Fabric only for now.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding
Simple Hammersfertig
simplehammers

Seven sledgehammers (Stone to Enderite) that mine a 3x3 area, reshape blocks into stairs and slabs, carve octets, crush diamond and quartz blocks and upgrade blocks in the world strike by strike, with the enchantments Break Through, Override and Radius, moved from SimpleBuilding (ids migrate). While it is loaded the in-world ways of the other Simple mods take the hammer instead of the axe.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Octets, Simple Crucibles, Simple Ores, jei
Simple Lightsfertig

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding
Simple Mobheadsfertig
simplemobheads

A head for every mob: dropped when a charged creeper's explosion kills it, worn for a small secret, placed, or played on a note block. Required sub-mod of SimpleBuilding; Simple Fun's and SimpleBuilding's heads moved in.

required SimpleLib, cloth_config
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Mobs, Simple End, Simple End Dragon Fight, jei, modmenu
Simple Octetsfertig
simpleoctets

Octets: half-block cubes (0.5 x 0.5 x 0.5) of every wood, every block with stairs and slabs and the melon, which mix in one cell, moved from SimpleBuilding (ids migrate). Cut in the stonecutter, crafted back into the block; the sledgehammer of Simple Hammers carves them. Octets of SimpleBuilding's and Simple End's blocks only with that mod.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple End, jei
Simple Ovensfertig
simpleovens

Reinforced, Netherite and Enderite furnaces, smokers and blast furnaces (2x/4x/8x speed, double experience from Netherite) and furnace minecarts, moved from SimpleBuilding (ids migrate). In-world upgrades with the axe on its own, with the sledgehammer of Simple Hammers.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple Foundry
Simple Foundryneu
simplefoundry

optional Simple Ovens
Simple Storagefertig
simplestorage

Crates (open wooden food boxes for every wood, hoppers and comparators work), the Storage Crafting Table (a crafting table that keeps its grid), tiered and trapped chests, trapped copper chests and tier shulker boxes (more slots, bigger stacks, upgraded in the world keeping their contents), moved from SimpleBuilding (ids migrate). Chest carts and chest boats, bundles, quivers, the placed bundle and the storage enchantments Drawer, Deep Pockets and Funnel (8c-1) also moved, then the four backpacks (8c-2).

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding, Simple End, trinkets_updated, curios

simplebeacons

Simple Challengesneu
simplechallenges

Game modes for a world of your choice. S0-S2: the Unlock mode - the world is cut into cells (a block column, a chunk, three chunks) and only free cells can be entered; every cell has a price worked out from the world (items, credits from advancements or levels), with deadlock protection and an emergency unlock. Presets are data files. Without a chosen challenge the mod changes nothing (safety lock). Needs SimpleLib; SimpleBuilding optional.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding

Simple Earthneu
simpleearth

Below everything stays Vanilla. Needs SimpleLib; SimpleBuilding optional.

required SimpleLib
optional SimpleBuilding

Simple Optimization: 3 Sub-Mods

Simple Optimizationneu
simpleoptimization

Super-mod without content of its own: bundles Simple Opt Render, Simple Opt Memory and Simple Opt Server with one on/off switch each.

required Simple Opt Render, Simple Opt Memory, Simple Opt Server
Simple Opt Memoryneu
simpleoptmemory

Memory optimizations (block states, models); stage 1 only holds the switch, config and mod detection.

Simple Opt Renderneu
simpleoptrender

Client optimizations (leaf culling, light-map cache, fast IP ping, telemetry off); each area steps back when a known optimization mod covers it.

Simple Opt Serverneu
simpleoptserver

Server optimizations: view distance follows server load (MSPT) within server.properties bounds; steps back for ServerCore.

    Super-mod without content of its own: bundles Simple Opt Render, Simple Opt Memory and Simple Opt Server with one on/off switch each.

    required Simple Opt Render, Simple Opt Memory, Simple Opt Server
    • Simple Opt Memory simpleoptmemory neu required

      Memory optimizations (block states, models); stage 1 only holds the switch, config and mod detection.

    • Simple Opt Render simpleoptrender neu required

      Client optimizations (leaf culling, light-map cache, fast IP ping, telemetry off); each area steps back when a known optimization mod covers it.

    • Simple Opt Server simpleoptserver neu required

      Server optimizations: view distance follows server load (MSPT) within server.properties bounds; steps back for ServerCore.

  • Simple Stats simplestats neu

    Every tool, weapon and piece of armor keeps its own statistics (blocks mined, kills by mob and variant, damage absorbed, distances, catches) with an item-specific statistics screen.
